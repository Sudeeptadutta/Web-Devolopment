if you’re new here, welcome to Write and Craft, a place for journalers, writers and stationery lovers. One thing you’ll learn quickly reading my blogs is that I love to journal. I love to write all sorts of different things each day. Mostly, I try to keep a little log of what I have been doing each day. When I get some extra time (which is rare as a mum, but I try!), I love to write new things in my journal. I love reading journal prompts online and love thinking of new and creative things to write in my journal, and I really love writing projects that make me think, reflect, and change my perspective.

I am currently using this writing journal. I love the lined writing journal because it allows me the complete freedom to write whatever I am feeling at the time.
